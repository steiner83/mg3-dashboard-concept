MG3 Custom now starts with the same factory-inspired layout as MG3 Original; select any instrument to change its design, size, color or position. Select a module to browse its styles, including lane assistance and hybrid energy visualizations. The hybrid indicators use adjustable simulated values. Use the advanced individual SVG colors editor to recolor every paint group in a design. New modules are placed outside the switchable panel. Drag a module into the 4 × 5 panel to assign it to the active page, or drag it out to make it static. Modules only move if their entire footprint fits. Drag the lower-right handle to resize. Every module snaps to the grid, respects a minimum size, and cannot overlap another module. Mandatory modules cannot be removed, but they can be moved. In Preview mode, all editing outlines disappear.

Prototype disclaimer: dimensions and safety requirements have not been verified by MG. This is a visual concept with simulated values, not an approved vehicle instrument cluster. Warning symbols and legally required information would need engineering and regulatory validation by MG.
